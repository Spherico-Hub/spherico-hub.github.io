## Optional Mods

Thanks to packwiz, you are able to turn on or off some of the mods, texture packs and shaders included in the modpack.
To change them:

<Steps>
  1. On Prism Launcher, launch the instance.
  2. Click on "Configure Mods"
  3. Select/Unselect the desired mods.
  4. Confirm your choices.
</Steps>

## Resource Packs

This is still Minecraft.

You can install [Faithful 32x](https://modrinth.com/resourcepack/faithful-32x) if 16 pixels isn't enough for your fancy tastes.
Several addons exist for it too, they can be found [here](https://faithfulpack.net/addons).

Since v4.1.3, [Fresh Animations](https://modrinth.com/resourcepack/fresh-animations) and some of its extensions are included in the modpack.

## Shaders

You'll need Iris in order for Shaders to work, which is already provided in the modpack.

Here are a few you can use, some of which are included in the modpack.

- For beefy rigs: [Rethinking Voxels](https://modrinth.com/shader/rethinking-voxels)
- Complimentary - [Reimagined](https://modrinth.com/shader/complementary-reimagined) or [Unbound](https://modrinth.com/shader/complementary-unbound)
- [Photon Shaders](https://modrinth.com/shader/photon-shader)
- [BSL Shaders](https://modrinth.com/shader/bsl-shaders)
